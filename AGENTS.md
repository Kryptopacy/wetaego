# WETAEGO — Omnichannel Multi-Business Operating System
Wetaego is a polymorphic, multi-tenant operating system powering unified commerce, scheduling, and catalog discovery across 6 core industry verticals:
1. **Dining / Food & Beverage** (menus, dietary tags, table service, bar tabs, kitchen routing)
2. **Wellness & Spas** (treatment menus, appointment scheduling, practitioner booking)
3. **Boutique & Retail** (fashion apparel, SKU variants, physical inventory ledger, size/color options)
4. **Hospitality & Hotel Stays** (room & suite reservations, date-range stays, room-service routing)
5. **Services, Repairs & Trades** (diagnostic intake, inspection requests, custom service quotes)
6. **Creator & Media Studios** (service rate cards, consulting packages, studio rentals, milestone deliverables)

Agents interacting with or modifying Wetaego must preserve this polymorphic architecture and never assume storefronts are dining-only.
